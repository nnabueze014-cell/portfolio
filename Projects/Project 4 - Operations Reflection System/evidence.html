Multi-Country Lead Generation & Prospect Research

Business Development • Lead Research • Market Research • Data Collection

Project Overview

As part of the Business Development team at PUEPR, I conducted structured lead generation research across multiple international markets. The objective was to identify small and growing businesses that met specific qualification criteria before they were introduced into the company's outreach pipeline. Unlike traditional lead generation, this project emphasized research accuracy, qualification standards, and data quality to ensure only relevant prospects were delivered to the Business Development team.

Business Challenge

The company needed a continuous pipeline of qualified businesses that could benefit from professional website development services. Research had to be performed consistently across multiple countries while ensuring each prospect satisfied predefined business rules. Poor-quality or incomplete data would reduce the effectiveness of future outreach campaigns.

My Responsibilities

  • Researched businesses using Google Maps and Google Search.
  • Generated up to 50 qualified business prospects daily.
  • Maintained weekly production targets of 250 qualified leads.
  • Verified business information before documentation.
  • Confirmed businesses met qualification requirements.
  • Collected structured business information into Google Sheets.
  • Organized prospects by country for easier campaign management.
  • Prepared clean datasets for Business Development outreach.

Qualification Criteria

  • Business rating of 3.5 stars or above.
  • No existing business website.
  • Valid contact information available.
  • Business operating within assigned countries.
  • Accurate category and location verification.

1. Lead Generation Task Requirements

This ClickUp task provided the operational framework for the research assignment. It specified daily production targets, weekly objectives, qualification criteria, approved business categories, and the countries assigned for research. Working from a clearly documented process ensured consistency and accuracy across all generated leads.

2. Multi-Country Prospect Database

Qualified businesses were organized in Google Sheets using structured fields including business name, category, rating, address, country, contact information, business links, website status, and generation date. Separate worksheets were maintained for each target country, enabling efficient organization and seamless handover to the Business Development team for outreach activities.
